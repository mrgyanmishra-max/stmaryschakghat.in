Director's Message

A Word from Our Director

Adv. Augustin Pullan

Director, Saint Mary H.S. School, Chakghat

Dear Students and Parents,

At Saint Mary Higher Secondary School, we believe that every child is unique and carries within them the potential to achieve greatness. Our mission is not just to impart knowledge, but to nurture character, build confidence, and shape responsible citizens of tomorrow.

Education is not merely about scoring marks — it is about learning to think, to question, to dream, and to work hard with honesty and dedication. I encourage every student to aim high, stay curious, and never stop learning.

Our dedicated team of teachers and staff is committed to providing a safe, supportive, and inspiring environment where every student can grow and shine. Together, let us build a brighter future for our children and our nation.

Warm Regards,
Adv. Augustin Pullan
